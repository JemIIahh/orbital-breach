## Playable pitch

**Orbital Breach: Eden Protocol** is a browser mech campaign about reopening Earth's machine-controlled gates to restore the last forest. Its central action is movement: shatter a station window, land on the exterior balcony, tether across the hull, fight through a city, and face the commander in Eden.

Three authored chapters, five distinct opponent classes, finite encounters, six threat tiers, relay upgrades, and a final commander give the prototype a beginning, progression, and an ending.

## Current delivery

Static Vite build, configured for Vercel, with no required environment variables. Controls use desktop keyboard and mouse. Art, textures, geometry, animation and sound are generated in code; Three.js and bundled fonts are credited in README and public licenses. There is no recorded voice acting or online multiplayer.

CPU simulation and scene tests verify the campaign path and combat behavior. GPU appearance, live controls, and frame rate still need hands-on browser playtesting.
